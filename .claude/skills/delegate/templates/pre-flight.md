A launcher starts a hands-off run's pre-flight from this file and fills each row for that run; the delegate skill's §4 says when. The rows, the checked sections and the example below are the current form.

Every decision this pre-flight or the run's morning report puts to the owner follows their decision pattern (CUSTOMISATION.md, Interaction preferences, "Decisions are asked in one pattern"): its high-level context first, then the options, then the recommendation with its reason, in plain words.

| Lane | Task | Seat | Model | Effort |
